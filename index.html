Website Information

Campus-Based Merchandise Ordering System for Enterprises is a web-based platform designed to provide a convenient and efficient way for students and faculty members to order merchandise from authorized campus enterprises. Similar to delivery applications such as Shopee and Foodpanda, the system allows users to browse stalls, view available products, add items to a cart, and place orders through a centralized digital platform. This system aims to reduce long lines at campus stores, improve the ordering process, and provide a faster and more organized way of managing merchandise transactions within the school.
